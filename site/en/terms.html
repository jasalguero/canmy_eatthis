The most important part: CanMy*EatThis gives general information, not veterinary advice. If your pet may have eaten something harmful, call your vet or an animal poison line straight away, even if the app says it knows of no problem. Don't wait for signs.

What the app is

CanMy*EatThis is a free app, made by Jose Salguero, that summarises what published veterinary and government sources say about foods, plants and products that dogs and cats might eat. Each answer shows its sources. The app is not a vet, does not examine your animal, and is not a medical device: it does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition.

Use it as a starting point only

  • An answer can be wrong or out of date, and “no known toxicity” or “not sure” never means something is harmless. Every animal is different, and amounts matter.
  • Product information from a barcode scan comes from Open Food Facts, a database written by volunteers, and can be incomplete or wrong. Check that the product shown is the one you have.
  • Phone numbers are checked before each release but can change. Services that charge a fee say so in the app.
  • Always follow your vet's advice over the app's.
