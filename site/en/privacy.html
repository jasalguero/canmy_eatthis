Privacy policy

CanMy*EatThis · Effective 29 September 2026

In short: CanMy*EatThis has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it does not send anything about you to us. Typed lookups work entirely on your phone. A few actions you choose — scanning a barcode, searching for an emergency vet, calling a hotline, or emailing us — contact another service, and this page says exactly what each one sends.

Who is responsible

CanMy*EatThis is a free app made and published by Jose Salguero, an individual, who is responsible for your data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Contact: canmy_eatthis@jasalguero.com.

What stays on your phone

The list of foods, plants and products the app checks against is built into the app. When you type what your pet ate, the answer is worked out on your phone, and what you typed is not sent anywhere.

The app saves a few things on your phone so they are still there next time: your language, region and appearance settings, whether you have seen the introduction, and what you are in the middle of typing. They never leave your phone. Deleting the app deletes them.

What leaves your phone, and only when you choose

When you… What is sent, and to whom
Scan a barcode The barcode number is sent to Open Pet Food Facts and, if it is not found there, to Open Food Facts, to look up the product and its ingredients. As with any internet request, these services also receive your phone's IP address. Nothing else about you is sent. Their own privacy policies apply to what they receive.
Tap “Find an emergency vet near me” The app opens a Google Maps search for emergency vets. Google's privacy policy applies from there, including to any location you let Google Maps use.
Tap a phone number The app opens your phone's dialler. The call is between you and the service you call; some services charge a fee, which the app states before you call.
Tap “Report a wrong answer” The app opens your own email app with a message ready to send. It contains the item, the answer shown, the animal, and the app and knowledge base versions — nothing about you. Nothing is sent unless you send it. If you do, we receive your email address and whatever you write.

What we do with a report

We use reports only to check and correct the app's answers, and to reply to you if needed. We do not add you to any list or share your email with anyone. We keep a report for as long as we need it to fix the problem it describes, and delete it on request.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the app's answers accurate (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)), and your choice to write to us.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking.
  • No photos: this version of the app does not use your camera roll, and the camera is used only to read barcodes.
  • We do not sell or share data, because we do not collect any.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, object to how we use it, or ask us to restrict its use. In practice the only personal data we can hold is an email you sent us. Write to canmy_eatthis@jasalguero.com.

You can also complain to the Spanish data protection authority, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, or to the authority where you live.

Children

The app is meant for pet owners and is not aimed at children. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If the app starts handling data differently, this page will change before that version is released, and the date at the top will show when.
